Live camera.

- Parent unit: "Open the live camera" shows the child's camera live (about 10 pictures per second) with live sound; both parents can watch at the same time
- Flash on/off (switches off by itself after 2 minutes), front/back camera, quality Low/Normal, rotate the picture
- Hold-to-talk button inside the camera screen; both parents can talk at the same time (their voices are mixed); live sound is muted on a phone while its button is held
- While someone watches, the child phone does not record clips; recording resumes about 2 seconds after the last viewer leaves
- Live view stops by itself after 15 minutes, when the parent screen closes or locks, or when the connection drops
- Child phone: asks for the camera permission (restart monitoring after allowing it)
