Stage 4 features: parent lock, alerts, sliders and delete everywhere.

- Parent phone lock: PIN (4 to 8 digits), fingerprint on Android 9 and newer; asked when the app opens and after 2 minutes away
- Child phone offline alert after 100 seconds without contact
- Child battery and temperature shown on the parent phone, with alerts when the battery is under 20% (not charging) or the temperature is over 42 °C
- Clip length 5 to 60 seconds
- Listen slider (0 to 15): 0 listens all the time; a number N checks every N seconds, listening for 5 seconds each time; a sound during a check is recorded in full
- Walkie-talkie volume slider is now on the parent phone
- Sound meter on the parent phone
- Deleting a recording removes it on every unit, including phones that are offline (done when they reconnect)
- Recordings older than 8 hours are deleted automatically on every unit
- New recording notification with a Play / Stop button
- Alerts follow the phone's ringer mode: silent = vibrate, normal = sound (and vibration if on), vibrate mode = vibration only; notification sound can be chosen in Settings
- Child screen: "Dark screen" button; touch the screen to wake it
- Pairing keys are encrypted with the Android Keystore (phones paired before this still work)
- Simple low-frequency filter: removes fan and motor rumble from the sound level (does not tell crying from other sounds)
